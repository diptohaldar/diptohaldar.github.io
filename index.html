01ABOUT

My work sits at the intersection of operations research, risk, resilience, and data-driven decision support.

I am an Industrial and Production Engineering graduate from Khulna University of Engineering and Technology (KUET), Bangladesh. My research interests include supply chain optimization, multi-hazard risk assessment, mathematical modeling, optimization under uncertainty, machine learning, and community resilience decision support.

My current research examines equitable disaster resource allocation under causal ambiguity, combining causal inference, partial identification, predictive analytics, and robust optimization.
