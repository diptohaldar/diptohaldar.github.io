Introduction

About Me

I am a Research Affiliate at the Risk-Informed Systems Engineering Lab (RISELAB) at Colorado State University Pueblo, supervised by Dr. Himadri Sen Gupta. My work sits at the intersection of operations research, causal inference, and disaster resilience — developing frameworks that support equitable and robust decision-making under uncertainty.

I completed my B.Sc. in Industrial and Production Engineering at Khulna University of Engineering and Technology (KUET) in 2025, where I developed a strong foundation in operations management, logistics, and quantitative modeling. My undergraduate thesis laid the groundwork for my current research directions.

My ongoing research addresses the challenge of equitable disaster resource allocation under causal ambiguity. Using Hurricane Ian evacuation data across 223 census tracts in Florida, I apply robust optimization combined with conformal prediction and counterfactual policy evaluation — contributing to more resilient, fair, and identification-aware emergency planning.
